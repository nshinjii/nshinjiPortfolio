    Questions

    Good questions.

    If yours isn't here, just send me a message.

    How fast can my site be live?

    A one-page Starter site is usually ready in about 3 days, and a Business site in about 10, once I have your logo, photos and details.

    What do I need to give you?

    Your logo if you have one, a few photos, your services and prices, and your opening hours. No logo or text? I can make those for you as an add-on.

    What if I don't like the design?

    Every plan includes revisions to the design we agree on: 2 on Starter and Business, and 3 on Pro. You check everything before it goes live.

    How does monthly work?

    There's a one-off setup fee, then the monthly price. There's a 12-month minimum, and after that it's month to month. If you'd rather pay once, the yearly option works out cheaper.

    Will people find me on Google?

    Every site gets proper page titles, descriptions and a sitemap so Google can read it. Business and Pro include local SEO, and Pro includes help with your Google Business profile.

    Who owns the website address?

    You do. A domain costs about £8–£16 a year. It stays in your name, and I set it all up for you.

    I already have a website. Can you redo it?

    Yes. I can rebuild it properly, and moving your existing site over starts from £48.
