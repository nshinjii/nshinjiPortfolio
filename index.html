    Questions

    How does monthly work?

    You pay a small setup fee to start, then the monthly price. There's a 12-month minimum, then it's month to month. Cancelling early means paying the remaining months.

    How does pay once work?

    One payment covers the build and 12 months of upkeep. Starter is paid in full up front. Creator and Pro are 50% to start and 50% before launch. Custom is paid in milestones. After the first year, renew upkeep yearly or switch to monthly.

    When does the clock start?

    Delivery times start once I have your text, photos and logo.

    What counts as a revision?

    Revisions cover the design we agreed. New pages or features are add-ons.

    Can I upgrade later?

    Yes, any time. You only pay the difference.

    Who owns the domain?

    You do. Domains cost about a year, you pay for it and it stays in your name. I set everything up.

    What if I stop upkeep?

    Take over your site files for a one-time handover fee.

    Will I know the price first?

    Always. You get a quote before any work starts.
