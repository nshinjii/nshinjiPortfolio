Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

This site is run by nshinji, a sole trader based in the UK. This page explains what information is collected when you use nshinjiportfolio.com and what happens to it. Questions? Email nshinjicontact@protonmail.com.

What I collect

  • What you send me. When you use the contact or order form, I get what you type in: your name, email, the details of your project and any username or number you add.
  • Messages. If you email or WhatsApp me, I keep the conversation so I can do the work and answer questions.
  • Visitor count (only if you accept). If you pick "Accept all" in the cookie banner, the site adds one to a public visit counter and a "here right now" counter. No name, email or device ID is attached to these counts.

Services the site uses

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) passes form messages to my inbox.
  • Abacus (abacus.jasoncameron.dev) runs the visitor counter, only after you accept it.
  • Google Fonts loads the site's font, which means your browser connects to Google.
  • Hosting. The company hosting the site may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Cookies and storage

The site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. It saves two small things in your browser: your cookie choice, and whether you picked light or dark mode. You can change your choice any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.

Why I use your information

To reply to you, quote for work, do the work you've paid for, and send invoices. I never sell your information or use it for marketing you didn't ask for.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that don't turn into work are deleted within 12 months. Records for paid work are kept for as long as UK tax rules require.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the information I hold about you, or object to how it's used. Email me and I'll sort it within a month. If you're not happy with the answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
